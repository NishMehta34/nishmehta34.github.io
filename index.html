MSc dissertation · 2026

Forecasting Great Britain's electricity demand with Transformers

Does a Transformer beat simpler models at short-term demand forecasting? I built a full pipeline on NESO National Demand data from 2001 to 2025, using the previous seven days (336 half-hours) to predict the next 24 hours (48 half-hours), and compared six approaches from naive baselines to XGBoost, LSTM and a Transformer.

On unseen 2023 to 2025 data, XGBoost came out ahead. The Transformer clearly beat the seasonal baselines and stayed competitive, but more model complexity did not buy better accuracy. The project ships with automated integrity tests and an interactive Streamlit dashboard.

No single model won every year. Lowest MAE by test year:
